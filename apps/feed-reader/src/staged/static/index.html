Crypta feed app

Feed Reader & Publisher

Declared permissions

  • content.fetch fetches configured feed sources through SDK helpers.
  • content.subscribe manages app-scoped USK subscription metadata.
  • content.insert.app-document publishes generated feed snapshots.
  • queue.read reads upload queue previews for published snapshots.
  • queue.write creates queue entries for published feed snapshots.
  • app.data.read restores the app-owned feed list and reader state.
  • app.data.write saves bounded feed, subscription, read-state, and draft metadata.

Safe-use notes

  • Feed sources, selected subscriptions, read state, and safe draft fields persist through app-data records.
  • Platform subscriptions store bounded metadata only; fetched feed bodies are not persisted.
  • Fetched content is rendered as text and no untrusted HTML is inserted into the page.
  • USK follow uses the platform scheduler instead of a browser-tab timer.
